How to print

  1. Print at 100% (“Actual size”), not “Fit to page”. The ruler at the bottom of the grid sheet must measure exactly 100 mm.
  2. Stick the notes inside the outlines, sticky edge at the top, lined up with the lines.
  3. Passes print only the picture inside the notes, without outlines.
  4. Feed the sheet top edge first, so the printer doesn’t lift the loose edge of a note.
  5. Before the first pass, mark the sheet with a pencil and check which side your printer prints on.
  6. After printing, give the ink half a minute to dry before peeling the notes off.

    In the combined PDF the first page is the grid and each next page is one pass. Print one page at a time.
